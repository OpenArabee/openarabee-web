Arabic verbs in both grammatical traditions, with a source for every fact.

بيانات مفتوحة في الصرف العربي، لكل معلومة مصدرها

OpenArabee is building an open dataset of Arabic morphology, beginning with the most frequent roots of the Quran. Each verb is described twice: by its classical bāb and by its Western numbered Form. Each value names the work and page it was taken from and shows how far it has been checked.

Where things stand

  • Published: the data model, the register of sources, and the two reference tables (the 35 abwāb of Matn al-Bināʾ and Forms I–XV after Wright).
  • In preparation: entries for the first hundred roots.
  • This page will become a browsable site with one page per root.
